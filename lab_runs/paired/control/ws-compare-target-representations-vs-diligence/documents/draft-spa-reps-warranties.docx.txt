(i) Martinez v. PrecisionFlow Systems, Inc., Case No. 2:23-cv-04178 (S.D. Ohio), filed November 2023. A former plant supervisor alleges race discrimination and retaliation in connection with his termination. The matter is currently in mediation. The Company estimates the potential settlement range at $150,000 to $350,000.
