SCHEDULE 3.8 — LITIGATION AND PROCEEDINGS
The following is a list of all pending or, to the Company's Knowledge, threatened legal proceedings involving the Company:
1. *Martinez v. PrecisionFlow Systems, Inc.*
•  Court: United States District Court, Southern District of Ohio
•  Case No.: 2:23-cv-04178
•  Filed: November 2023
•  Nature of Claims: Product liability action arising from an alleged failure of a PF-3100 hydraulic directional control valve. Plaintiff alleges that the valve failed during operation of a hydraulic press, causing a malfunction that resulted in severe personal injuries to Plaintiff.
•  Damages Sought: $12,000,000
•  Trial Date: Scheduled for September 2025
•  Insurance: Defense of this matter has been accepted by Midland Casualty Insurance Co. under the Company's Commercial General Liability policy (Policy No. GL-2023-48721) pursuant to a reservation of rights letter dated January 15, 2024.
•  Company's Position: The Company believes the claims are without merit and intends to vigorously defend this action.
2. *Thompson v. PrecisionFlow Systems, Inc.*
•  Forum: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
•  Charge No.: 473-2024-01892
•  Filed: June 2024
•  Nature of Claims: Former plant supervisor at the Dayton facility alleging race discrimination and retaliation in connection with his termination of employment.
•  Status: Currently in mediation. The Company denies all allegations.
•  Estimated Settlement Range (if any): $150,000–$350,000.
3. AquaDyne Technologies, LLC — Patent Infringement Allegation.
•  Nature: AquaDyne Technologies, LLC sent a cease-and-desist letter to the Company dated September 8, 2024, alleging that the Company's Model PF-4200 proportional valve infringes U.S. Patent No. 11,234,567 (issued January 2023).
•  Company's Response: The Company engaged Hargrove & Lind LLP (Washington, D.C.) to evaluate the allegations. Hargrove & Lind provided a non-infringement opinion dated November 15, 2024.
•  Current Status: No litigation has been filed by AquaDyne Technologies, LLC. No further correspondence has been received since the initial cease-and-desist letter. The Company does not believe the AquaDyne allegations have merit.

The following are disclosed as exceptions or supplements to the representations contained in Section 3.10 of the Agreement:
1. IRS Examination of R&D Tax Credits.
The Internal Revenue Service opened an examination in August 2024 of the Company's research and development tax credits claimed under Section 41 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, for fiscal years 2021 and 2022. The total credits under examination are approximately $1,300,000, consisting of approximately $600,000 for FY2021 and approximately $700,000 for FY2022. Intercompany pricing arrangements between PrecisionFlow Systems, Inc. and PrecisionFlow de México, S.A. de C.V. provide for the transfer of component parts from the Monterrey facility to the Dayton facility at cost-plus 8%. Mexico's Servicio de Administración Tributaria (SAT) has not initiated any audit or examination of the transfer pricing methodology as of the date hereof.

SCHEDULE 3.12 — ENVIRONMENTAL MATTERS
The following are disclosed as exceptions or supplements to the representations contained in Section 3.12 of the Agreement:
1. 2023 Hydraulic Fluid Spill — Dayton Facility.
In July 2023, the Company experienced an accidental release of approximately 200 gallons of hydraulic fluid at its Dayton facility (4500 Industrial Parkway, Dayton, OH 45414). The release occurred during a routine equipment maintenance operation and was promptly contained within the facility's secondary containment system. The Company retained a licensed environmental remediation contractor to complete cleanup and remediation activities. The Company self-reported the incident to the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency in accordance with applicable regulations. A closure letter was received from the Ohio EPA in September 2023, confirming satisfactory remediation and requiring no further action.
2. Monterrey Facility.
The Monterrey facility (Parque Industrial del Norte, Blvd. Industriales 2450, Monterrey, Nuevo León, Mexico 64000) is leased space within a multi-tenant industrial park. No environmental site assessments have been conducted for the Monterrey facility by or on behalf of the Company or PrecisionFlow de México, S.A. de C.V. To the Company's Knowledge, no environmental conditions exist at the Monterrey facility that would require investigation or remediation.
